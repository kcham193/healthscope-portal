The travel-time + Bayesian catchment pipeline currently runs only for Tanzania. The same methodology (modelled 100 m travel-time surface from the MAP 2015 friction raster, constrained WorldPop 2026 population, and a Bayesian gravity catchment model with travel-time-decayed attendance probability) will roll out to the other seven countries as country-specific inputs become available.

In the meantime, per-country facility distribution and coverage are visible in Registry Explorer.
